# SalesAnalysis

Exploratory analysis of a year of e-commerce sales data in pandas: from merging monthly files and cleaning the data to answering concrete business questions.

## Business questions answered

1. What was the best month for sales and how much was earned?
2. Which city sold the most products?
3. What time should ads be displayed to maximise the likelihood of a purchase?
4. Which products are most often sold together?
5. Which product sold the most, and why?

## Steps

- Merging 12 monthly CSV files into one dataset
- Cleaning: dropping empty rows, fixing data types
- Feature engineering: month, city and hour columns
- Aggregations and charts for each question

## Stack

Python · pandas · matplotlib · Jupyter
